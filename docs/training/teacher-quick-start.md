# StudyMondo Teacher Quick-Start

**Free for teachers. No ads. About 15 minutes to set up.**
Full guide: **studymondo.com/help/getting-started-teachers**

---

## 1. Turn on teacher features (1 min)

1. Go to **studymondo.com/for-teachers**.
2. No account? Choose **Sign up free to get started**.
3. Tick **I confirm I'm a teacher or educator**.
4. Choose **Activate my free teacher account**.

## 2. Create your class (1 min)

1. Open **My Classes** in the menu.
2. Choose **New Classroom** and name it (for example, "Period 3 AP Calculus").
3. Open the class.

## 3. Add students (2 min)

Pick one:

- **Join code:** students go to **studymondo.com/join-class** and type it.
- **Join link or QR code:** the code fills in for them. Put the QR code on your projector.
- **Roster import:** **Roster › Import students**. Paste emails or upload a CSV (`name, email`). Students sign in with that school email.

## 4. Give a diagnostic (day 1)

- **SAT or MCAT:** **Insights › Class plan › Assign diagnostic**. Everyone gets the same test.
- **Other courses:** send the course's diagnostic link (**Start with the free diagnostic** on the course page).
- Students need a free account. Each one gets a personal study plan.

## 5. Read the results (day 2)

Open **Insights**:

| View | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Class plan** | The topics your class most needs, ranked |
| **Performance** | Mastery and exit-quiz results per student. **Export CSV** is here |
| **Gradebook** | Every assignment score in one grid |
| **Standards** | Class mastery by AP unit, SAT domain or MCAT category |
| **Engagement** | Who logs in, and daily flashcard habits |

## 6. Assign work (ongoing)

1. **Work › Assignments** › new assignment.
2. Choose **Interactive Lesson** for a topic. Students do the lesson, then the exit quiz.
3. Keep the target at **80%**. That is the site-wide pass mark for clearing a topic.

## 7. Teach live (optional)

- **Class game:** **Class Lobby** on your dashboard. Choose Teams or Free-for-all, and turn on Chaos Mode if you like. Students join at **studymondo.com/competitive/join**.
- **Live lesson:** in your class, **Live class sessions**. Conference (on camera) or Webcast (YouTube).
- **First webcast?** Turn on YouTube live streaming the day before. It takes about 24 hours to activate.

## 8. Every day: Needs Attention

Your dashboard lists students who missed a due assignment, scored below
target, or have been inactive for 14 days. Choose **Mark as seen** once
you've handled it.

---

### How a student's week works

**Diagnostic → study plan → lesson → exit quiz (80% clears it) → flashcards daily → retake the diagnostic**

- Taking a topic's exit quiz, after the lesson, adds that topic's flashcards to the student's deck.
- Joining your class starts a **class flashcard deck**. Their personal deck keeps every card.
