Earning a 5 on AP Calculus AB is achievable with the right strategy. Here is a comprehensive breakdown of what the exam tests, how to prepare, and the most effective techniques top scorers use.

## Understanding the Exam Format

The AP Calculus AB exam consists of two sections:

| Section | Format | Questions | Time | Weight |
|---------|--------|-----------|------|--------|
| Section I Part A | Multiple Choice (no calc) | 30 | 60 min | 33.3% |
| Section I Part B | Multiple Choice (calculator) | 15 | 45 min | 16.7% |
| Section II Part A | Free Response (calculator) | 2 | 30 min | 16.7% |
| Section II Part B | Free Response (no calc) | 4 | 60 min | 33.3% |

## The Big Three: Core Concepts

### 1. Limits and Continuity (~10-12% of exam)

Limits are the foundation of calculus. You must be fluent in:

- Evaluating limits algebraically (factoring, rationalizing, L'Hôpital's Rule)
- Understanding one-sided limits and limits at infinity
- The formal definition of continuity
- The Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)

**Pro tip:** IVT questions appear on nearly every exam. If a function is continuous on [a, b] and you need to show a value exists between f(a) and f(b), cite IVT explicitly.

### 2. Derivatives (~40-50% of exam)

Derivatives dominate the exam. Master these in this order:

1. **Definition** — The limit definition of the derivative
2. **Rules** — Power, product, quotient, chain rules
3. **Implicit differentiation** — For curves not written as y = f(x)
4. **Related rates** — Setting up and solving word problems
5. **Applications** — Optimization, linear approximation, Mean Value Theorem

**Common mistake:** Students forget that the chain rule applies when differentiating composite functions. When in doubt, ask: "Is there a function inside another function?"

### 3. Integrals (~30-40% of exam)

Build your integral skills systematically:

1. **Definite integrals** as accumulated change
2. **Fundamental Theorem** — Both parts
3. **U-substitution** — The most common integration technique
4. **Area between curves** — Set up the correct integral
5. **Volume** — Disc/washer and shell methods

## Free Response Strategy

FRQ questions are worth 50% of your score. Here is how to maximize points:

### Show Your Work

AP readers award points for **process**, not just answers. Write out every step:

- State the formula or theorem you are using
- Show substitution steps
- Include units when applicable
- Box or circle your final answer

### The "Justify" Rule

When a question says "justify your answer," you must:

1. State the mathematical reason (theorem, definition, or test)
2. Show how the conditions are met
3. State the conclusion

Example: "Since f'(x) changes from positive to negative at x = 3, f has a relative maximum at x = 3 by the First Derivative Test."

## Calculator Tips

For calculator sections, know how to:

1. Graph a function and find intersections
2. Evaluate a definite integral numerically
3. Find zeros of a function
4. Calculate a derivative at a point

Store commonly needed programs but remember — the non-calculator sections test whether you truly understand the math.
