You have **four weeks** to prepare. This plan builds cumulatively: Week 1 solidifies AB foundations, Week 2 adds BC techniques, Week 3 emphasizes series and convergence, and Week 4 refines with full-length exams and final review.

Aim for **60-90 minutes per weekday, 2-3 hours on weekends.** Consistency beats cramming.

## Week 1: AB Foundations (Limits through Integrals)

| Day | Topics | MCQ | Practice | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Limits, continuity, IVT | 10 | 2 IVT problem setups | Single-sided limits; when IVT applies |
| **Tue** | Derivative rules, chain rule | 12 | Chain rule on 5 composite functions | Speed over accuracy initially |
| **Wed** | Implicit differentiation, related rates | 8 | 2 related-rates FRQ setups | Identify the changing quantities |
| **Thu** | Optimization (application of derivatives) | 10 | 1 full optimization FRQ | Must cite First Derivative Test |
| **Fri** | Antiderivatives, basic integration | 12 | $u$-substitution on 6 integrals | Memorize $\int e^{kx}$, $\int \frac{1}{x}$, etc. |
| **Sat** | Area between curves, volume (disks) | 8 | 1 area FRQ + 1 volume FRQ | Units! Always include units. |
| **Sun** | Review + Week 1 practice set | 15 MCQ | 2 mixed FRQ (any AB topic) | 90 min review total |

| **Sun** | Review + Week 2 practice set (mixed) | 20 MCQ | 2 FRQ (1 AB, 1 parametric/polar) | 90 min; assess weak topics |

## Weekly milestone targets

- **Week 1 end:** 85%+ on AB MCQ (limits, derivatives, basic integrals).
- **Week 2 end:** 80%+ on mixed AB + parametric/polar MCQ.
- **Week 3 end:** 75%+ on series/convergence MCQ (this is the hardest topic).
- **Week 4, Thu-Sat:** Full mock scoring 70+ (out of 108).

## Common patterns to lock in by Week 2

1. **FTC with chain rule:** $\frac{d}{dx} \int_a^{g(x)} f(t) \, dt = f(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$.
2. **Parametric $dy/dx$:** Always $\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$, never $\frac{dy}{dx}$.
3. **Polar area:** Always $\frac{1}{2} \int r^2 \, d\theta$, not $\int r^2 \, d\theta$.
4. **Speed formula:** $\sqrt{(dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2}$, not $(dx/dt) + (dy/dt)$.

## Common patterns to lock in by Week 4

5. **Series convergence:** Ratio test for $a^n$ or $n!$ in denominator; $p$-test for $\frac{1}{n^p}$; alternating test for $(-1)^n a_n$.
6. **IOC endpoint testing:** **Every exam tests endpoints.** Test them separately; convergence can differ at endpoints.
7. **Lagrange error:** $|R_n| \leq \frac{M|x-a|^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}$. Find $M$ on the interval, then compute the bound.
8. **Integration by parts:** LIATE (Logs, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential). Choose $u$ as the priority item.
