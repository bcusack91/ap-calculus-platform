## Must-know derivatives

| Function | Derivative |
|---|---|
| $x^n$ | $n x^{n-1}$ |
| $\sin x$ | $\cos x$ |
| $\cos x$ | $-\sin x$ |
| $\tan x$ | $\sec^2 x$ |
| $\sec x$ | $\sec x \tan x$ |
| $e^x$ | $e^x$ |
| $\ln x$ | $1/x$ |
| $a^x$ | $a^x \ln a$ |
| $\arcsin x$ | $1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ |
| $\arctan x$ | $1/(1+x^2)$ |

**Chain rule:** $(f(g(x)))' = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$.
**Product rule:** $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$.
**Quotient rule:** $(u/v)' = (u'v - uv')/v^2$.

## Theorems you may have to cite by name

- **Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT):** $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$, $k$ between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ ⟹ ∃ $c \in (a, b)$ with $f(c) = k$.
- **Mean Value Theorem (MVT):** $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ AND differentiable on $(a, b)$ ⟹ ∃ $c$ with $f'(c) = \frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$.
- **Extreme Value Theorem (EVT):** $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ ⟹ $f$ attains an absolute max and min on $[a, b]$.
- **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC):**
  - Part 1: $\frac{d}{dx} \int_a^x f(t) \, dt = f(x)$.
  - Part 2: $\int_a^b f'(x) \, dx = f(b) - f(a)$.

## Application formulas to know cold

- **Average value:** $\bar f = \frac{1}{b-a} \int_a^b f(x) \, dx$.
- **Area between curves:** $\int_a^b (\text{top} - \text{bottom}) \, dx$.
- **Volume by disks:** $V = \pi \int_a^b [R(x)]^2 \, dx$.
- **Volume by washers:** $V = \pi \int_a^b \left([R(x)]^2 - [r(x)]^2\right) dx$.
- **Volume by cross sections (squares):** $V = \int_a^b [s(x)]^2 \, dx$.
- **Distance traveled:** $\int_a^b |v(t)| \, dt$.
- **Displacement:** $\int_a^b v(t) \, dt$.
- **Speed:** $|v(t)|$.

## Top 10 traps that cost real points

1. **Forgetting "+ C"** on indefinite integrals.
2. **Distance vs displacement** — distance uses $|v(t)|$.
3. **Chain rule with FTC:** $\frac{d}{dx} \int_a^{g(x)} f(t) \, dt = f(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$. Don't forget $g'(x)$.
4. **MVT hypotheses** — must say *continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$*.
5. **Justifying max/min** — must cite First or Second Derivative Test.
6. **Units missing** on real-world FRQ answers (liters, meters, seconds, etc.).
7. **Riemann sum from a table** — read the question carefully: left, right, midpoint, or trapezoid?
8. **Implicit differentiation** — every $y$ becomes $\frac{dy}{dx}$ via the chain rule.
9. **Speeding up vs slowing down** — speeding up: $v$ and $a$ same sign.
10. **Decimal rounding** — at least 3 places on calculator section.

## Calculator quick reference

- **Numerical derivative at a point:** `nDeriv(f(x), x, value)`
- **Definite integral:** `fnInt(f(x), x, a, b)`
- **Find intersection:** Graph both, then `2nd → CALC → intersect`.
- **Find a zero:** Graph, then `2nd → CALC → zero`.
- **Solve $f(x) = k$:** Either graph $f(x) - k$ and find zero, or use `nSolve`.

## During the exam

1. **MCQ:** Mark and skip anything that takes >90 seconds. Come back.
2. **No-calc MCQ:** Use process of elimination aggressively. The wrong answers are usually computational sign-flips.
3. **FRQ:** Read all 6 questions first. Start with the one you're most confident on.
4. **FRQ:** Always write the integral or derivative *expression* before the answer.
5. **Don't leave anything blank** on FRQs. Set up the integral, define your variables — partial credit adds up.
