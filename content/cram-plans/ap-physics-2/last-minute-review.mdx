## Essential formulas by unit

### Fluids
$$P = P_0 + \rho g h \quad \text{(pressure at depth)}$$
$$F_b = \rho_{\text{fluid}} V_{\text{displaced}} g \quad \text{(buoyant force)}$$
$$A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2 \quad \text{(continuity)}$$

### Thermodynamics
$$PV = nRT \quad \text{(ideal gas law, } R = 8.31 \, \text{J/(mol·K))}$$
$$\Delta U = Q + W \quad \text{(first law, } W = \text{work on gas)}$$
$$W = P \Delta V \quad \text{(isobaric process)}$$
$$\eta = 1 - \frac{Q_c}{Q_h} \quad \text{(heat engine efficiency)}$$

### Electric Force & Potential
$$F = k \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} \quad \text{(Coulomb's law, } k = 9 \times 10^9 \, \text{N·m}^2\text{/C}^2 \text{)}$$
$$E = k \frac{Q}{r^2} \quad \text{(electric field)}$$
$$V = k \frac{Q}{r} \quad \text{(electric potential)}$$
$$W = q \Delta V \quad \text{(work on charge)}$$

### Circuits
$$V = IR \quad \text{(Ohm's law)}$$
$$R_{\text{series}} = \sum R \quad \text{(series resistors)}$$
$$\frac{1}{R_{\text{parallel}}} = \sum \frac{1}{R} \quad \text{(parallel resistors)}$$
$$P = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R} = IV \quad \text{(power)}$$

### Magnetism & Induction
$$F = qvB \sin\theta \quad \text{(Lorentz force)}$$
$$F = BIL \sin\theta \quad \text{(force on current-carrying wire)}$$
$$\Phi_B = BA \cos\theta \quad \text{(magnetic flux)}$$
$$\mathcal{E} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} \quad \text{(Faraday's law)}$$

### Optics
$$n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2 \quad \text{(Snell's law)}$$
$$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{d_o} + \frac{1}{d_i} \quad \text{(lens/mirror equation)}$$
$$m = -\frac{d_i}{d_o} \quad \text{(magnification)}$$
$$\text{Bright fringes: path difference} = m\lambda \quad \text{(double-slit)}$$

### Modern Physics
$$E = hf \quad \text{(photon energy)}$$
$$hf = KE_{\text{max}} + \phi \quad \text{(photoelectric effect)}$$
$$E = mc^2 \quad \text{(mass-energy equivalence)}$$
$$N(t) = N_0 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/t_{1/2}} \quad \text{(radioactive decay)}$$

## Top 15 traps that cost real points

| # | Trap | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Work sign in thermo** | Compression: $W > 0$ (on gas). Expansion: $W < 0$ (by gas). *Write out which.* |
| 2 | **Series vs parallel** | Series: $R = \sum R$, voltage divides. Parallel: $\frac{1}{R} = \sum \frac{1}{R}$, current divides. *Redraw simplified first.* |
| 3 | **Lenz's law direction** | Induced current opposes the *change*, not the field. If flux ↑, induced field ↓. |
| 4 | **Lens equation sign** | Real: $d > 0$. Virtual: $d < 0$. Converging: $f > 0$. Diverging: $f < 0$. |
| 5 | **Lorentz force is perpendicular** | $\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B}$ always ⊥ to $v$. Right-hand rule: fingers = $v$, curl = $B$, thumb = $F$. |
| 6 | **Photoelectric threshold** | Photon energy must *exceed* work function. If $hf < \phi$, zero electrons emitted. |
| 7 | **Snell's law at normal incidence** | $\theta = 0°$ → $\sin(0°) = 0$ → no bending at all. |
| 8 | **Buoyancy = displaced volume** | $F_b$ depends on fluid volume displaced, not total object volume. |
| 9 | **Potential vs potential energy** | $V$ = energy per charge (J/C). $U = qV$ = total energy. Don't confuse. |
| 10 | **Magnification sign** | Negative $m$ = inverted. Positive = upright. Check your diagram matches. |
| 11 | **Capacitor in series** | $\frac{1}{C_{\text{tot}}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2}$ (opposite of resistors). |
| 12 | **Half-life counting** | After 1 half-life: $N = N_0/2$. After 2: $N = N_0/4$. Count carefully. |
| 13 | **Forgetting $k$ in Coulomb** | $F = k \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$. Missing $k$ → off by $9 \times 10^9$. |
| 14 | **Diagram labels** | Rubrics give points for *drawing itself*. Label all forces, currents, rays. |
| 15 | **Units missing** | Final answer without units = partial or no credit. Always write (N, J, V, A, etc.). |

## Diagram quick rules

### Circuits
✓ Do: Nodes (•), battery polarity, current arrows, $R$ labels, simplified equivalent.
✗ Don't: Ambiguous junctions. Unlabeled components.

### Ray diagrams
✓ Do: 3-ray method, focal points, optical axis, object left/image right (real) or left (virtual).
✗ Don't: Rays that don't meet at image. Forgetting to mark $F$.

### PV diagrams
✓ Do: Label points, identify process type, shade area under curve (= work).
✗ Don't: Forget to show direction (arrow). Mislabel isothermal as straight line.

### Free body diagrams
✓ Do: All forces, dot for object, labels ($F_b$, $F_g$, $F_{\text{applied}}$).
✗ Don't: Include forces that don't act. Skip direction arrows.

## Calculator tips

- **Show setup first:** Always write the equation before the decimal. Rubrics award points for method, not just answer.
- **Numerical solve:** Use `nSolve` or graph-intersect on messy algebra, but show your setup.
- **Scientific notation:** Watch tiny exponents (e.g., $10^{-19}$ on charges). One order-of-magnitude error fails the whole problem.
- **Unit check:** Before submitting, ask "does this unit make sense?" (Force in N, not J; Energy in J, not N).

## During the MCQ section (first 50 questions, 90 min)

1. **Skim all 50 first (2 min).** Mark scary ones.
2. **Do confident questions first (2–3 min each).** Build momentum.
3. **Flag & skip anything taking >3 min.** Return in final 5 min.
4. **Last 5 minutes:** return to flagged. Use process of elimination (cross out violations of physics).

> **💡 Elimination trick:** Cross out answers that violate a principle (e.g., negative mass, $F$ without acceleration). Usually leaves 2–3 choices.

---

## During the FRQ section (6 FRQs, 90 min)

1. **Read all 6 first (3 min).** Pick the 3 you're most confident on.
2. **Do those 3 first (12 min each = 36 min).** Lock in easy points.
3. **Attempt harder 3 (6 min each = 18 min).** *Partial credit adds up*—set up equation, define variables, show work.
4. **Do NOT leave blank.** A wrong attempt with reasoning earns 1–2 points. Blank = 0.

> **⚠️ FRQ rubric:** Typically 1 pt setup, 1 pt equation, 1 pt calculation, 1 pt reasoning. Hit setup + equation = 50% already.
