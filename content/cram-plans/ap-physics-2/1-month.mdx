## Week 1: Fluids & Thermodynamics (5 hrs)

### Monday–Tuesday: Fluids (2 hrs)
- **Hydrostatic pressure**: $P = \rho gh$, gauge pressure.
- **Buoyancy equilibrium**: $F_b = \rho_{\text{fluid}} V_{\text{displaced}} g$, floats when $F_b = F_g$.
- **Continuity**: $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$.
- **Practice**: 8 MCQs + 1 worked example (float equilibrium).

### Wednesday: Thermodynamics Part 1 (1.5 hrs)
- **Ideal gas law**: $PV = nRT$.
- **State variables**: $P$, $V$, $T$, $n$.
- **Processes**: isobaric, isochoric, isothermal. Work only in isobaric: $W = P\Delta V$.
- **Practice**: 8 MCQs, sketch PV diagram.

### Thursday–Friday: Thermodynamics Part 2 & First Law (1.5 hrs)
- **First Law**: $\Delta U = Q + W$ ($W$ = work on gas).
- **Internal energy**: $\Delta U = nC_V \Delta T$.
- **Heat engines**: $\eta = 1 - \frac{Q_c}{Q_h}$.
- **Practice**: 9 MCQs + 1 FRQ (identify processes, find $W$, $Q$, $\Delta U$).

### Week 1 Quiz (1 hr)
- **25 MCQs** (fluids & thermo mixed, 12 min per topic).
- **2 FRQs**: one buoyancy, one PV diagram.
- **Target**: ≥ 16/25 MCQs (64%), both FRQs ≥ 2/3 points.

> **💡 Checkpoint:** If you score &lt;14 on MCQs, rewatch buoyancy and ideal gas law videos before Week 2.

## Week 2: Electric Fields & Circuits (5 hrs)

### Monday: Coulomb & Electric Fields (1 hr)
- **Coulomb**: $F = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$ ($k = 9 \times 10^9$).
- **Field**: $E = k\frac{Q}{r^2}$.
- **Field lines & superposition**.
- **Practice**: 8 MCQs, sketch field lines.

### Tuesday: Electric Potential (1 hr)
- **Potential**: $V = k\frac{Q}{r}$.
- **Equipotentials** ⊥ to field.
- **Work**: $W = q\Delta V$.
- **Practice**: 8 MCQs + 1 worked example.

### Wednesday–Thursday: Circuits Part 1 (1.5 hrs)
- **Ohm's Law**: $V = IR$.
- **Series**: $R_{\text{tot}} = \Sigma R$, voltage divides.
- **Parallel**: $\frac{1}{R_{\text{tot}}} = \Sigma \frac{1}{R}$, current divides.
- **Power**: $P = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R} = IV$.
- **Practice**: 14 MCQs, sketch equivalent resistances.

### Friday: Circuits Part 2 & Kirchhoff (1.5 hrs)
- **KCL**: $\Sigma I_{\text{in}} = \Sigma I_{\text{out}}$.
- **KVL**: $\Sigma V = 0$ around loop.
- **Multi-loop**: apply both laws simultaneously.
- **Practice**: 1 multi-loop FRQ + 1 power FRQ.

### Week 2 Quiz (1 hr)
- **30 MCQs** (E-fields, potential, circuits).
- **3 FRQs**: Coulomb/field, series/parallel, multi-loop Kirchhoff.
- **Target**: ≥ 19/30, ≥ 6/9 FRQ points.

> **⚠️ Trap:** Kirchhoff looks complicated but is systematic. Write *every* loop and node equation. Most students skip and guess—don't.

## Week 3: Magnetism & Optics (5 hrs)

### Monday–Tuesday: Magnetic Force & Flux (1.5 hrs)
- **Lorentz force**: $F = qvB\sin\theta$, right-hand rule.
- **Wire force**: $F = BIL\sin\theta$.
- **Flux**: $\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta$.
- **Practice**: 10 MCQs + 1 worked example.

### Wednesday: Faraday & Lenz (1 hr)
- **Faraday**: $\mathcal{E} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$.
- **Lenz**: induced current opposes *change* in flux.
- **Practice**: 8 MCQs + 1 FRQ.

### Thursday–Friday: Optics (1.5 hrs)
- **Snell**: $n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2$.
- **Lens/mirror**: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{d_o} + \frac{1}{d_i}$, $m = -\frac{d_i}{d_o}$.
- **Ray diagrams**: 3-ray method.
- **Wave optics**: double-slit $\Delta = m\lambda$, thin-film $2nt = m\lambda$, polarization.
- **Practice**: 12 MCQs + 1 lens FRQ.

### Week 3 Quiz (1 hr)
- **30 MCQs** (magnetism, induction, optics).
- **3 FRQs**: Faraday, lens/mirror, wave.
- **Target**: ≥ 19/30, ≥ 6/9 FRQ points.

## Week 4: Modern Physics & Full Diagnostics (6 hrs)

### Monday–Tuesday: Modern Physics (1.5 hrs)
- **Photoelectric**: $hf = KE_{\text{max}} + \phi$.
- **Atomic**: energy levels, transitions.
- **Mass-energy**: $E = mc^2$.
- **Nuclear**: $N(t) = N_0 (1/2)^{t/t_{1/2}}$.
- **Practice**: 10 MCQs + 1 FRQ.

### Wednesday: Cumulative Review (1.5 hrs)
- Skim [last-minute review](/courses/ap-physics-2/cram/last-minute-review).
- Revisit one weak unit.
- 1 review FRQ.

### Thursday–Friday: Full Diagnostics (3 hrs)
- **Mock Exam 1 (90 min)**: full 50-MCQ, strict timing.
- **Mock Exam 2 (90 min)**: 6 real past FRQs, timed.
- Grade & identify weak spots.

### Week 4 Targets
- **Mock MCQ**: ≥ 65% (32/50).
- **Mock FRQ**: average ≥ 60%.
- **Overall**: trending ≥ 70% (solid 5 trajectory).

---

## By end of Week 4

- **≥ 65%**: Solid foundation. Next week: weak units only.
- **55–64%**: Focus on high-yield topics (circuits, optics, thermo).
- **&lt; 55%**: Request tutoring. Gaps too large to close solo.
