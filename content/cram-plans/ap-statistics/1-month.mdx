A month of focused, structured prep is more than enough to **earn a 4 or 5** on the AP Statistics exam — even if your in-class grade is shaky. This plan assumes ~1 hour per weekday and ~2-3 hours on each weekend day.

If you need to compress: see our [7-day cram plan](/courses/ap-statistics/cram/7-day) or [3-day cram plan](/courses/ap-statistics/cram/3-day).

## Week 1: Data Exploration + Sampling and Experiments

**Goal:** Master the descriptive statistics and study design vocabulary that show up on every FRQ.

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Diagnostic: take 25-question MCQ pretest | List your 5 weakest topics |
| Tue | Univariate displays + 5-number summary, IQR, outlier rule | 12 problems |
| Wed | Center, spread, comparisons, transformations of data | 10 problems |
| Thu | Scatterplots, $r$, LSRL, residuals, $r^2$ | 12 problems |
| Fri | Sampling methods (SRS, stratified, cluster, systematic), bias | 10 problems |
| Sat | Experiments vs observational studies, confounding, blocking, blinding | 12 problems + 1 study design FRQ |
| Sun | Mixed practice + review missed problems | 20 MCQs |

**Internal link:** [AP Statistics topic library →](/courses/ap-statistics).

## Week 2: Probability + Random Variables + Sampling Distributions

**Goal:** Get comfortable with discrete and continuous distributions and the conceptual basis of inference.

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Probability rules, conditional probability, independence | 12 problems |
| Tue | Discrete random variables: $E(X)$, $\text{Var}(X)$, combining variables | 10 problems |
| Wed | Binomial and geometric distributions | 12 problems |
| Thu | Normal distribution + `normalcdf` / `invNorm` | 10 problems |
| Fri | Sampling distribution of $\hat p$ + Large Counts condition | 10 problems |
| Sat | Sampling distribution of $\bar x$ + Central Limit Theorem | 10 problems + 1 random variables FRQ |
| Sun | Mixed Units 3-5 review | 25 MCQs |

> **💡 The key insight:** Sampling distributions are NOT about a single sample's data — they're about the distribution of statistics across many possible samples. This conceptual move is what enables every CI and test.

## Week 3: Inference — Confidence Intervals and Significance Tests

**Goal:** Lock in the 4-step framework and all 8 inference procedures.

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 1-prop $z$-interval and 1-prop $z$-test | 12 problems |
| Tue | 2-prop $z$-interval and 2-prop $z$-test | 10 problems |
| Wed | 1-sample $t$-interval and 1-sample $t$-test | 12 problems |
| Thu | 2-sample $t$-interval and 2-sample $t$-test | 10 problems |
| Fri | Paired $t$-interval and paired $t$-test (when to use which) | 10 problems |
| Sat | Type I/II errors + Power | 8 problems + 1 CI FRQ |
| Sun | 4-step FRQ practice — write out State, Plan, Do, Conclude verbatim | 2 timed FRQs |

> **⚠️ FRQ trap:** When asked to interpret a confidence interval, NEVER say *"the probability the true mean is in this interval is 95%."* Instead: *"We are 95% confident the true mean is between X and Y [units]."* The 95% refers to the procedure, not this specific interval.

## Week 4: Chi-Square + Regression Inference + Mock Exam

**Goal:** Finish the curriculum and build exam stamina.

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Chi-square goodness-of-fit | 8 problems |
| Tue | Chi-square test for independence/homogeneity | 8 problems + 1 chi-square FRQ |
| Wed | LinReg $t$-test and CI for slope; reading computer output | 8 problems |
| Thu | **Mixed multiple-choice mock**: 40 MCQs in 90 min | Score and review |
| Fri | **Full timed FRQ section**: 6 FRQs in 90 min | Score with rubric |
| Sat | **Full timed mock exam** (3 hours total) | Honest score |
| Sun | Targeted review of weakest mock topics + light FRQ work | Don't cram new material |

## Daily habits throughout the month

- **Spaced repetition flashcards** for inference procedures and conditions — 5 min/day.
- **One FRQ per week minimum** from a [past College Board exam](/courses/ap-statistics) — score with the rubric.
- **Track missed problems** in a single notebook by topic. Review weekly.

## Calculator routine (build into Week 2 onward)

Become fluent with these on your specific calculator (TI-84, TI-Nspire, etc.):
1. `normalcdf` and `invNorm`.
2. `binomcdf`, `binompdf`, `geomcdf`.
3. `1-PropZInt`, `1-PropZTest`, `2-PropZInt`, `2-PropZTest`.
4. `TInterval`, `T-Test`, `2-SampTInt`, `2-SampTTest`.
5. `χ²-Test` and `χ²GOF-Test`.
6. `LinRegTTest`.
7. `Stat → Edit` for L1/L2 + `1-Var Stats` for summary statistics.

## The night before & morning of

- Skim the [last-minute review checklist](/courses/ap-statistics/cram/last-minute-review).
- Review the [FRQ template phrasings](/courses/ap-statistics/cram/frq-practice).
- Sleep 8+ hours.
- Eat breakfast with protein and slow carbs.
- Pack: 2 sharpened #2 pencils, blue/black pens, approved calculator, fresh batteries, photo ID.

## Final word

A 4-week plan rewards consistency more than intensity. Show up every day, even if it's just 30 minutes. The students who score 5s aren't the ones who pull all-nighters — they're the ones who logged 25 quiet sessions in a row.
