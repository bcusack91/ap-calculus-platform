# How to Study with Flashcards - Quick Guide

## ✅ Your Flashcard System is Now Active!

You now have a complete Anki-style flashcard system that automatically creates flashcards as you learn!

## 🎯 How to Use It

### 1. **Complete Topics to Generate Flashcards**

When you finish a topic (like completing Part 1 of Reflection & Refraction), the system:
- ✨ Automatically generates 5-10 flashcards from the content
- 📚 Extracts key equations, constants, and definitions
- 🎴 Shows you a notification: "8 flashcards created!"
- 💾 Saves them to your personal study deck

### 2. **Access Your Flashcards**

There are THREE ways to study your flashcards:

#### Option A: Review Dashboard (Recommended)
- Go to **`/flashcards/review`**
- See stats: Total cards, cards due today, new cards
- Click "Start Review Session"
- Study with spaced repetition algorithm

#### Option B: Browse by Topic
- Go to **`/flashcards`**
- Browse all topics
- Click on any topic to study its flashcards
- Good for reviewing specific topics

#### Option C: Direct Links
- Look for "🎴 Practice with Flashcards" links after completing topics
- Click to immediately review that topic's flashcards

### 3. **Review Your Cards with Spaced Repetition**

When reviewing:
1. **Read the question** on the front
2. **Think of the answer**
3. **Click to flip** the card
4. **Rate your recall:**
   - **Again**: Didn't remember (see it again soon)
   - **Hard**: Remembered with difficulty (shorter interval)
   - **Good**: Correct with some thought (standard interval)
   - **Easy**: Perfect recall (longer interval)

The system automatically schedules when you'll see each card again!

### 4. **Build Long-Term Memory**

- **Daily reviews**: Even just 5-10 minutes helps!
- **Be honest**: Accurate ratings make the algorithm work better
- **Cards you struggle with**: Will appear more frequently
- **Easy cards**: Will appear less often (weeks/months later)

## 📊 Understanding Your Stats

In the review dashboard (`/flashcards/review`), you'll see:

- **Total Cards**: All flashcards you've created
- **Due Now**: Cards ready to review today  
- **New Cards**: Never reviewed before
- **Review Cards**: Previously seen, now due

## 🎓 Pro Tips

1. **Complete topics regularly** → More flashcards automatically added
2. **Review daily** → Best for memory retention
3. **Don't cram** → Trust the spaced repetition algorithm
4. **Use hints** → Available if you're stuck on a card
5. **Check your streak** → Review counts help track progress
