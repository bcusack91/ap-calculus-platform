# SEO Acceleration Checklist

## Google Search Console Setup

1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add your property (your domain)
3. Verify ownership (add meta tag to your site or DNS record)
4. Submit sitemap: https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml

### Sitemap URLs to Submit:
- /sitemap.xml (main sitemap)
- /courses-sitemap.xml (if you have one)
- /topics-sitemap.xml (if you have one)

### Request Indexing for Top Pages:
Use "URL Inspection" tool to request indexing for:
- Homepage
- Top 20-30 most important course pages
- Top 20-30 most valuable topic pages

**Tip**: You can manually request indexing for ~10 URLs per day

## Red Flags to Avoid for AdSense

1. **Don't apply too early** - wait until you have:
   - ✅ 20+ well-written pages (you have 534!)
   - ✅ Site looks professional
   - ✅ Gets some organic traffic (even 20-50 visitors/day helps)

2. **Don't have:**
   - ❌ Broken links
   - ❌ Under construction pages
   - ❌ Lorem ipsum placeholder text
   - ❌ Poor mobile experience
   - ❌ Slow load times

## Post-Approval Optimization

Once AdSense is approved:
- Place ads strategically (between sections, not overwhelming)
- Test ad positions (top of page usually performs best)
- Enable auto ads initially to learn optimal placements
- Monitor CPM/RPM in AdSense dashboard
- Typical education site CPM: $2-$8 (US traffic)
- With good traffic: 1000 daily visitors = $50-200/month
